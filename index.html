      Off the clock

      Outside of research, I’m usually looking for somewhere new to explore. I’ve been surfing for ten years, spend as much time as I can camping and backpacking, and have a particular soft spot for Yosemite. Lately I’ve been getting into off-roading, and one of my goals is a road trip through as many national parks as I can. I’ve traveled to six countries, from visiting family in Estonia to spending time in Dublin, and those experiences have made me curious about the different ways people experience and make sense of the world. I’m drawn to unfamiliar places, new perspectives, and the little details that make people and their environments unique.
